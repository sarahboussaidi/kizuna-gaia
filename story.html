Chapter 一  ·  Story

Two farmers, two hemispheres, the same question.

Same rain. Same slope. Two answers.

How do you keep water on a hillside that wants to let it go? In the south of Tunisia, the answer was carved into the valleys centuries ago. On the slopes of rural Japan, it was carved too — in a different language, with different hands.

Chapter 1 of 7

Same rain.
Same slope.
Two answers.

Tunisia built dikes to hold the water back. Japan built steps to let it fall slowly. Neither village ever heard of the other.

Gaia was born from that gap. Not an app that tells farmers what to do, but a translator between two agricultural memories — reading your land, then finding the technique that already solved your problem somewhere else on Earth.
