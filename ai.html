    How this works, honestly

    The engine on this page is a scoring function written by hand: a small table of practices, each with the conditions it needs, scored against what you selected. It is a demonstration of the reasoning shape we want, not a trained model. A real version would read soil, elevation and weather data and would be able to say “I do not know”.
