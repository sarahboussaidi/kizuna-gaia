Chapter 7 of 7
تونس M

Mounir · illustrative

Olive grower — Matmata, Tunisia

Rebuilt three jessour with spill sills after a match with a farmer in Nagano. Lost no soil in the December storms.

“I did not know my grandfather's wall had a cousin.”

日本 K

Kenji · illustrative

Rice farmer — Nagano, Japan

Shared forty years of terrace maintenance notes. Learned mulching with olive pruning in return, for his own dry summers.

“The sun is harsher there. The idea still travels.”

Amel · illustrative

Oasis cooperative — Tozeur, Tunisia

Mapped the three growing layers of the oasis into Gaia so the satoyama model could be compared against it.

“Our oasis is a satoyama with palms.”

“Translation is not the hard part. Context is.”
